About

This site collects public data on the Illinois criminal justice system that is published but hard to use: dozens of Excel workbooks from the Illinois Department of Corrections and thousands of monthly report sheets from the Administrative Office of the Illinois Courts. Two pipelines gather those files, clean them into consistent tables and keep them current. The results are four interactive dashboards — prisons, parole, adult probation and juvenile probation — with rates per 100,000 residents, trends over time and racial disparities, and the code behind all of it.

I lead the CCJDA unit at the Illinois Criminal Justice Information Authority, where I build automated data pipelines and analytics tools and manage the projects and data-sharing agreements around them. My background is in Mathematics and Environmental Economics. This site is a personal project presenting non-confidential work, built only on data the agencies publish openly.
